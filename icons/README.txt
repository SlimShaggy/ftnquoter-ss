To create icons:
1. Open create-icons.html in a browser
2. Icons will download automatically as PNG files
3. Place them in this directory as icon-48.png, icon-64.png, icon-96.png and icon-128.png

The icon-*.svg files are the same design in vector form.
The icons show "FQ" under two white lines on a blue background, with a small red cursive "SS" subscript.
