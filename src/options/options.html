About

ftnQuoter v2.0
FTN-style message quoter for modern Thunderbird
Original by Sergey Poziturin, modernized for Thunderbird 115+

This extension applies FidoNet-style quoting (like GoldEd) to your replies. Instead of standard ">" prefixes, it uses initials like "AB>" for better readability.
